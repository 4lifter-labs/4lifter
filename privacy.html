4Lifter – Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the 4Lifter mobile application ("4Lifter", "the App") handles information.

Please also review our Use & Disclaimer, which govern your use of 4Lifter.

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Summary

  • The App works offline and has no user accounts.
  • Your workouts, training history, personal records and settings are stored only on your device.
  • We do not collect, receive, sell or share your personal data.
  • The App contains no advertising and no analytics.
  • You can remove all your data at any time by uninstalling the App or clearing its storage.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

  • Workout templates you create or import
  • Training history and workout records
  • Exercises you create
  • Personal maximum lifts and statistics
  • App settings and preferences
  • A temporary draft of the workout in progress

This information is created by you and stays on your device. We have no access to it.

3. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number or account details.

We do not collect location data, contacts, advertising identifiers, device identifiers, analytics data or crash reports.

The App does not include third-party advertising, analytics or tracking tools.

4. Permissions the App Uses

  • Notifications: Used to display workout timers and workout information.
  • Foreground Service & Wake Lock: Used to keep workout timers running while the screen is off or the App is in the background.
  • Sound & Vibration: Used only for rest timer alerts.
  • File Access: Used only when importing or exporting backup files selected by the user.

None of these permissions are used to collect, transmit or share personal data.

5. Export, Import and Sharing

When you export a backup, the App creates a file on your device.

If you choose to share that file, it is sent only to the app or service you select (for example, email, messaging apps or cloud storage services).

4Lifter does not receive, store, process or have access to exported files.

6. Android Backup

4Lifter disables Android automatic cloud backups.

Your workouts, training history and settings are not automatically backed up to Google Drive or any remote server.

If you wish to preserve your data, use the Export Backup feature.

7. Third-Party Services

4Lifter does not integrate any third-party service that receives, stores or processes your workout data or personal information.

When you download or update the App through Google Play, Google may process information about you under Google's own Privacy Policy.

8. Retention and Deletion

Your data remains stored on your device until you choose to remove it.

Uninstalling the App or clearing its storage permanently deletes all stored data.

Because 4Lifter does not store any copy of your data, deleted information cannot be recovered.

If you wish to keep your workout history, personal records and settings, please export a backup before uninstalling the App.

9. Security

Data stored by 4Lifter is kept within the App's private storage area and is protected by Android's application sandbox.

No method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

We recommend protecting your device with a screen lock, password, PIN or biometric authentication.

Files that you export or share outside the App are no longer protected by 4Lifter.

10. Children

4Lifter is not intended for children under the age of 13.

The App does not require user accounts and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Your Rights

Because 4Lifter does not collect, receive, store or process personal data, there is generally no personal information held by us that can be accessed, corrected, deleted or transferred.

You remain in full control of your data because it is stored locally on your device.

12. Future Changes to Data Handling

If we add optional features that send data off your device, such as cloud sync or user accounts, this Privacy Policy will be updated before those features are released.

Where required by law, we will obtain your consent before transmitting personal information outside your device.

13. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

The latest version will always be available on this page together with the date of the most recent update.
